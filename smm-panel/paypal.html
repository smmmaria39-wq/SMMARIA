Frequently Asked Questions

How to create a PayPal account in Uganda?

To create a PayPal account in Uganda, visit the PayPal website, choose Personal or Business account type, enter your email, create a password, fill in your personal details, and link a card or bank account. SMMARIA Panel offers ready-made verified PayPal accounts starting at $5.00 for personal and $15.00 for business accounts.

What is the difference between PayPal Personal and Business accounts?

A PayPal Personal account is for individual use — sending money to friends, online shopping, and receiving personal payments. A PayPal Business account is for merchants and businesses — it includes invoicing, payment buttons, API access, multi-user access, and business reporting. Business accounts can accept credit card payments from customers who don't have PayPal.
