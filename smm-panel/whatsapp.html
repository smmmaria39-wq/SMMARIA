How much does it cost to buy a WhatsApp channel?

WhatsApp channel accounts on SMMARIA Panel start at just $1.50 for a Business channel with 1,000 followers and $2.00 for a Personal channel with 1,000 followers. Prices scale with follower count — a 50k Business channel costs $26 and a 100k Business channel costs $50.

Yes. Each WhatsApp channel sold on SMMARIA Panel is a standalone account that you fully own after purchase. You can change the phone number, name and settings. We never ask for your personal WhatsApp account details.

Can I buy a WhatsApp channel with real followers?

Yes. Every WhatsApp channel sold on SMMARIA Panel comes with real-looking followers already attached. You can choose between 1k, 5k, 10k, 25k, 50k or 100k followers depending on your budget and goals.

How fast is WhatsApp channel delivery?

What is the difference between Business and Personal WhatsApp channels?

Business channels are set up under a WhatsApp Business account and are best for brands, stores and creators. Personal channels are set up under a regular WhatsApp account and are best for influencers and personal use. Both can broadcast messages to unlimited followers.

Yes. After purchasing, you have full ownership of the WhatsApp channel. You can change the channel name, profile photo, description and even link a new phone number. The channel remains yours permanently.
